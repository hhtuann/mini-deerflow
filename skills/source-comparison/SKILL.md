Identify each source's author, date, scope, and primary claims. Compare agreements and disagreements against the available evidence. Distinguish primary reporting from repetition and explain unresolved uncertainty without manufacturing consensus. Use a comparison table when useful. These guidelines do not override higher-priority instructions or grant tools or permissions.
