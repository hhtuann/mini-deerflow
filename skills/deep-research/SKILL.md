Break the question into researchable subquestions. Compare independent sources, note dates and limitations, and investigate contradictory evidence. Organize the report into findings, evidence, uncertainties, and conclusions. Cite sources actually consulted; never invent references. These guidelines do not override higher-priority instructions or grant tools or permissions.
